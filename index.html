VaultHolders (Personal Finance & Investing Education)

About the Brand

VaultHolders is a Personal Finance & Investing Education brand. It focuses on teaching people how money really works, breaking down the market, mentoring people through investing, and helping them build wealth that lasts generations.

The Challenge

VaultHolders' social media page was relatively quiet, with little consistent content going out. The founder wanted to make the page more visible, educate the audience about personal finance and investing, and create more opportunities for people to engage with the brand.

The challenge was time. Researching topics, coming up with content ideas, writing scripts and captions, publishing, and keeping up with the audience all took time the founder didn't have. My job was to take those day-to-day content responsibilities off his plate and create a more consistent presence for the brand.

What I Did
  1. Researched the Brand & Audience

    I got familiar with the brand, its audience, and the financial questions they were likely to have. I looked at their interests, common questions, pain points, and where the brand could provide useful information.

  2. Researched Competitors

    I reviewed other finance and investing pages to understand what was already being discussed, how similar brands communicated, and where VaultHolders could approach topics differently.

  3. Built the Social Media Strategy

    I used the research to build a strategy around the page's main goals: increasing visibility, making finance and investing easier to understand, and encouraging meaningful engagement.

  4. Developed Content Pillars

    I created content pillars based on the audience's questions, problems, interests, and relevant conversations in the finance and investing space. This gave the page a clearer direction instead of relying on random content ideas.

  5. Researched & Simplified Market Topics

    I turned market news and financial topics into content that was easier to understand. I used sources including Nairametrics and Investopedia, then translated the information into simple, beginner-friendly content.

  6. Planned & Managed Content

    I planned the content calendar and created educational and engagement-focused posts. I also handled captions, scheduling, publishing, and audience interactions.

  7. Reviewed Performance

    At the end of each month, I reviewed performance to see what was getting attention and where there was room to improve. I used those insights to adjust the content direction and planning for the following month.

Results

Within the first month, VaultHolders became more active and visible on social media, with all growth coming organically and without paid promotion. Impressions more than doubled, rising from 31.8K to 68.2K.

  • Engagement rate: 1.8% to 2.8%
  • Likes: 269 to 1.2K
  • Replies: 47 to 224
  • Reposts: 57 to 128
  • Bookmarks: 66 to 139

The growth in replies, reposts and bookmarks meant people were doing more than viewing the content: they were responding, sharing, and saving it for later. Profile visits rose from 141 to 181, and by the end of the first month the page had gained 66 new followers while reaching more people and maintaining a more consistent presence online.

Personal Brand (Social Media Management & Content Strategy)

About the Brand

This is my own social media manager page, which I started to document my journey, showcase my skills, build an audience, and create opportunities to work with clients.

The Objective

I was starting from zero, so I wanted to build visibility for myself while figuring out what kind of content my target audience would actually respond to.

Since I was also building my portfolio, I used my own page to put what I was learning into practice, test different content ideas, and track what worked.

What I Did
  1. Built a Content Strategy

    I created a content strategy around my target audience, their interests, and the kind of content I believed would be useful to them.

  2. Created the Content

    I developed content ideas and wrote captions for the page, making sure each post had a clear purpose.

  3. Engaged with the Community

    I actively engaged with people and accounts in the social media space. This helped me build connections, get my content in front of more people, and learn what others were discussing.

  4. Tested Different Content Formats

    I tested formats including carousels and static posts to see how people responded and which formats helped the page get more visibility.

Results

Within three weeks, I grew the page from 0 to 100+ followers. Views also increased from 400 to 11,000+ during the same period.
